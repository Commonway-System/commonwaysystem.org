A short set of plain-language questions about a specific street or intersection, how people use it, how fast traffic moves through it, what's around it, will eventually turn a reader's answers into the Functional Classification and Street Typology that fits.

That tool isn't built yet. In the meantime, browse [Street Types & Classifications](/guide/street-types/) directly and match your street to the closest description, or look through [Patterns](/patterns/) by Functional Classification if you already have a rough sense of whether you're dealing with a Local street, a Collector, an Arterial, or an intersection.

:::tip
This page is planned but not yet written. Check the [Release History & Roadmap](/about/release-history/) for progress.
:::
