## How the guide is organized

Every pattern sits inside one of four Functional Classifications: Local, Collector, Arterial, or Freeway, plus a fifth cross-cutting set for Intersections and Crossings. Within a classification, patterns are grouped by purpose-based Street Typology.

## Scale hierarchy

Patterns exist at one of five scales: Network, Corridor, Segment or Intersection, Facility, and Element. A pattern's ID encodes its scale and context, for example `STR-UMS-014`.
