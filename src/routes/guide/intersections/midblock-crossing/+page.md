Suburban is an edge case. Undeveloped and Rural are unlikely pairings.

## Design notes

Midblock crossings supplement, rather than replace, intersection-level crossing treatment. They concentrate at higher density, where longer blocks and denser pedestrian destinations make a corner-only crossing pattern insufficient on its own.
