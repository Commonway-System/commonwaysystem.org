The Guide is where you start when you have a problem, not a curiosity. If you already know you're after a Local Alley or a Turbo-Roundabout, go to [Patterns](/patterns/) and look it up. If you're trying to figure out why the street outside your house feels dangerous, what it's supposed to be called, or how to actually get something changed, start here.

Patterns describes what a good street looks like. The Guide is for deciding what to do about the one you've got: which pattern fits your situation, and how to move it through the process that actually builds or changes a street. That second part, the part where a plan turns into pavement, runs through your local government: whoever authorizes the change, approves the budget, and schedules the work. Most people never learn how that works, which is a large part of why streets that everyone agrees are unsafe can sit unfixed for years. The Guide is meant to close that gap, in plain language.

This section is built in three parts:

- **Foundations** — the vocabulary and classification system used throughout the rest of the Commonway System. Start here if terms like Functional Classification, Street Typology, or pattern scale are new to you.
- **Find Your Pattern** — a short diagnostic that turns a plain description of a street into the pattern that fits it.
- **Make It Happen** — a walkthrough of how local road decisions actually get made, who to ask, and how to push one forward.

Foundations is built out. Find Your Pattern and Make It Happen are both still being written, each has a placeholder page in the sidebar for now, describing what will eventually be there.
