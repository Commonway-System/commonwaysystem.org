## Pattern technical reference

Every pattern page in this guidebook carries two kinds of backup: numbered citations at the bottom of the page, which back specific claims, and this section, which will hold the fuller technical detail beneath a pattern's plain-language description.

A pattern page tells you what a Street Typology is and when to use it. The technical reference holds the layer beneath that: exact dimensions, formulas, design criteria, and specification tables, the detail a reader needs when they're actually drawing or building something, not just deciding what to build.

This is different from a page's References list. References back a specific claim with a source. This section expands on the claim itself with the numbers and criteria a citation alone doesn't carry.

Entries will follow the same Pattern ID used throughout the guidebook. None are published yet. Until a pattern has one, the pattern page itself is the complete, current guidance.
