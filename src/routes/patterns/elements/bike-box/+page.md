## Why this pattern exists

At a signalized intersection, a cyclist stopped alongside or behind queued vehicles has reduced visibility to drivers and a harder time making a turning movement across traffic once the signal changes; a bike box gives cyclists a visible head-start position ahead of the queue, addressing this specific intersection conflict. This pattern exists to document where and how that positioning treatment is applied.

## Key considerations

- Requires a "no turn on red" restriction for vehicles at that approach, since the box’s function depends on vehicles not entering the space during the cyclist’s queuing period
- Most effective when paired with a bike lane leading into the box, so cyclists have a legible, protected path to reach it
- Signal timing and driver awareness both affect real-world compliance; signage and clear markings help reinforce expected behavior
