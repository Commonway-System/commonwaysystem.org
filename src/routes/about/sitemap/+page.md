## Other ways to browse

- [Search](/search/): search every Pattern, Retrofit Strategy, Guide page, and media item, with filters. Also opens from the Search button in the header, or Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac).
- [Text-only pages](/text/all-pages/): a plain, unstyled version of every page with no images, scripts, or animation.
- [XML sitemap](/sitemap.xml): the list of pages for search engines.
- [llms.txt](/llms.txt): an index of the guidebook's pages as markdown, for AI tools.
- [llms-full.txt](/llms-full.txt): the whole guidebook as one markdown file, for AI tools.
